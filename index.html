Before you book

Does the first session include assessment and treatment?

Yes. The first session includes clinical assessment, initial treatment, and a clear next-step plan.

What if I've already been told to rest or stop training?

That may be part of a phase, but it should not automatically be the end of the process. The question here is when and how to return well.

Do I need an X-ray or MRI before booking?

No. Clinical examination is the primary diagnostic tool. If you have existing imaging, bring it — it helps, but it is not required.

I've tried physio before and it didn't last. Why would this be different?

The most common reason: treatment reduced the pain without rebuilding the capacity. When the load returned, the pain returned. Here, assessment identifies the deficit and treatment builds it back.

I'm crossing from the U.S. — does that work?

Yes. Zona Río is approximately 30 minutes from the border, and sessions are conducted in English.
