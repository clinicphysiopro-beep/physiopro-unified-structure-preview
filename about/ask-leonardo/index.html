What a real answer looks like

A sample exchange.

Patient question

"I've had lower back pain for two years and have done physical therapy before with no lasting results. Every time I return to training the pain comes back. Is this actually something that can improve?"

Leonardo's response

That pattern — pain clears, then comes back when load returns — usually means the original treatment addressed the pain but didn't rebuild the capacity. It's not how it has to be.

Two years of recurrence gives us a baseline. First session: assess what's actually weak, overloaded, or compensating. From there we start building it back — not managing it. Send your question and I'll tell you honestly whether this is the right fit for your situation.

Leonardo Machado, LFT · PhysioPro · Zona Rio, Tijuana
